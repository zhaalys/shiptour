Chana–Thepha, Songkhla

Caring for the Sea That Feeds a Community

"In Chana and Thepha, small-scale fishers use local knowledge to care for marine habitats and sustain the food their families depend on. Their conservation work supports healthy seas and recognition of communities' role in managing their coast."

Kudat, Sabah

Communities Shaping the Future of Sabah's Coast

"In Kudat, coastal communities restore coral, protect turtles and support women's participation in community life. They are also asking for a stronger voice in development decisions affecting their homes and livelihoods around Sabah's Tun Mustapha Park."

Bohol

Bohol Fishing Families Stand for Climate Justice

Sira & Manggroholo, Papua

Learning from Papua's Forest Guardians

"In Sira and Manggroholo, Indigenous communities shared forest knowledge with young people at the Forest Defender Camp. The gathering brought people together to learn from local forest guardians and exchange experiences of protecting their land and way of life."
